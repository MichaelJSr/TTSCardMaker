# Taking Your Turn

It's time to put your creatures to use!
Every creature gets one ACTION when their turn starts. The action can be used to ATTACK, MINE, or to put the creature into DEFENSE MODE.

## ATTACK
![A fox dashing with its sword](fox-dash.webp){.right width=15}
A creature can choose any creature on the playing field to ATTACK. Attacking removes HP from the chosen creature equal to the ATTACK VALUE of the attacking creature, minus the DEFENSE VALUE of the creature being attacked. (See [“ATTACKING”](#/rules/attacking) for more info)

## MINING
![Steve with his pickaxe](steve.webp){.right width=11}
A creature can choose to MINE, generating BRICKS equal to its STAR LEVEL for the player (*see [FIELD CARDS](#/rules/field-cards) for more*)

## DEFENSE MODE
![A fox behind its shield](fox-shield.webp){.left width=13}
A creature can use its action to go into DEFENSE MODE, doubling its defense while it is in this state. It goes out of defense mode at the beginning of its next turn. This state is singified by turning the creature 90 degrees.

### TURN PHASES {.banner}

## PHASE 1
After it has been decided which slot's turn it is, (see: [“The Global Turn”](#/rules/the-global-turn)), the slots turn begins and (BOT) effects Activate.

## PHASE 2
This is the main part of a turn.
- May use (OYT) abilities.
- May now use items from the hand.
- May now swap facedowns to and from the hand.
- May now buy from the shops, and shuffle the item shop.
- May use actions.
- May swap primary and secondary fields if no actions have been used. (See: [“THE FIELD”](#/rules/the-field))

## PHASE 3
- (ENDT) effects activate.
- Can no longer do anything you could in phase 2.

## PHASE 4
The turn is now over.
- Status Effects deal damage/heal, duration ticks down by 1.
