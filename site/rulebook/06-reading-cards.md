# Reading Cards

## READING CREATURES
![A creature card and its counters, numbered](reading-creatures.webp)
1. TYPES: Special markers that give creatures certian effects. (See: [Creature Types](#/rules/creature-types))
2. STAR LEVEL: A creature's star level. A higher star level means the creature is more powerful.
3. From top to bottom: HEALTH, DEFENSE, ATTACK, and SPEED counters. These counters are responsible for showing the current value of a creature's stats.
4. Max Health, Defense, Attack, and Speed bars.
   A. The health value is double of what is shown.
   B. The defense value is half of what is shown.
5. ABILITIES: Text explaining what a creature is capable of doing while they are on the field.

HEALTH: A creature perishes when its health reaches zero.
DEFENSE: Subtracted from any attack damage this creature takes.
ATTACK: The amount of damage this creature deals to opponents.
SPEED: Determines the order that creatures take their turn in (More about turn order in [GLOBAL TURNS](#/rules/the-global-turn)).

## READING ITEMS
![An item card, numbered](reading-items.webp){width=45}
1. TYPES: Dictate how the item functions. (See: [“ITEM TYPES”](#/rules/item-types))
2. STAR LEVEL: An items star level. The more stars means the item is more powerful. The star level is how much the item costs to play in star points. (See: [“ITEMS”](#/rules/items))
3. EFFECTS: Text explaining the effect the item has when it is played.
