# Death

## ON DEATH
- When a friendly creature's HP reaches 0 it dies: its “On Death” effects proc (does not apply to creatures not on the field), it loses all status effects and all of its items are unequipped.
- If something revives it on the spot (such as a [[Life Shroom]], or an (OD) ability that revives it or switches it to its next form), it is not defeated.

## DEFEAT
- A creature that dies and isn't revived on the spot is **defeated**, and goes to the graveyard on your side of the field.
- When a prime creature is defeated: your team gains points equal to that creature's star level, all non-persistent minions in that slot die, and the slot is empty so a new prime creature can be placed in it.
- Outside of “On Death” effects, anything that says a prime creature “dies” means it is defeated.

## REVIVES
![A ghostly fox](fox-ghost.webp){.right width=26}
- Creatures that are revived from “On Death” effects only yield Points when they are defeated.
- For creatures revived by *other means*, Points are kept from it being defeated, but defeating the revived version awards 0 Points.
- When a creature dies and is revived, its HP and all of its stats, abilities, charge and counters are reset to their base, unless otherwise specified.

## SPECIFICS
- Creatures composed of multiple targets only award points when all targets are defeated.
- A creature being “destroyed” instantly by any effect takes true damage equal to its current HP.
- If a creature that has used an action is defeated mid-turn, the creature that replaces it can't use actions, but may use an ability.
