# Minions

## INTRODUCTION
- Minions are creatures that when summoned are attached onto a parent prime creature.
- Minions are always attached to their parent creature, and stick with them even if the parent creature is moved or swapped into the hand.
- A minion's parent creature is the creature it is on, not always its summoner. A summoner can occasionally summon minions onto other creatures. Minions are tied to their parent creature, not their summoner.
- For most purposes, Minions are considered creatures, and have their own action, but they do not yield points when defeated. They do go to the graveyard when defeated.
- Every minion and parent creature in the primary field may use their actions (See: [“THE FIELD”](#/rules/the-field)) in whatever order the player choses.
- Minions cannot mine for BRICKS.
- If Parent creature is defeated, all minions in the slot die (excluding persistent minions)
- 5 star minions cannot be targeted by minion effects.
- If something just says “summon”, it means onto the creature that the ability is from.

## TYPES
[offensive_minion]:Offensive minions occupy the same field as their parent creature. (See: [“FIELDS”](#/rules/the-field))
[defensive_minion]:Protective Minions always occupy whatever field the parent creature and offensive minions are not in. When summoned, these push the parent creature (and the offensive minions attached to it) back to the secondary field, and are placed in the primary field. As a creature can only act in the primary field, This typically cannot act on the same turn as a parent creature, as they can never both occupy the primary field. (See: [“THE FIELD”](#/rules/the-field))
- If a protective minion is summoned after a parent creature has already used an action that turn, it does not gain an action for that turn.

## PERSISTENT
If a minion is “persistent”, when its parent creature is defeated, it will be attached to the next prime creature that gets placed into the slot. If there is no creature to fill the slot, it occupies the slot alone, and must be killed for the game to be won by the opponent.
