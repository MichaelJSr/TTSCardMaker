# Table

![The table: the main decks and the developer decks, numbered](table-main.webp)

## MAIN DECK
1. The CREATURE DECKS. 5 star Creatures are in the leftmost deck, 4 Stars are next, then 3 Stars, then 2 Stars.
2. The ITEM DECKS. 4 Star Items are in the leftmost deck, 3 Stars are next, then 2 Stars, then 1 Stars.
3. FIELD CARDS for the Field card Shop (See: [“SHOPS”](#/rules/shops)). 4 Stars are in the leftmost deck, 3 Stars are next, then 2 Stars, then 1 Stars.
4. N.M.E. Items for the N.M.E. Shop. (See: [“SHOPS”](#/rules/shops))

## DEVELOPER DECK
5. The Developer Version of the creature decks. This is a reduced version of the full deck, meant to only included newer or recently changed cards that require more playtesting. For most players you can ignore this but you can play with it if you want a more wacky experience!
6. “Baseline” creatures that we mix into the Developer version. These cards are considered balanced, so they serve as a way to test the less balanced cards.

=== page ===

![The counters and the shops, numbered](table-counters.webp)

::: panel
1. The Global Turn counter. Starts at 1, and ticks up by 1 once every filled slot on the field has taken a turn. (See: [“THE GLOBAL TURN”](#/rules/the-global-turn))
2. Star Point counter. At the beginning of each global turn, this is set to 7. These are spent in order to play items. Items cost their star value in star points in order to play them. (See: [“ITEMS”](#/rules/items))
3. BRICKS counter. BRICKS are used to buy Field cards. Field cards cost 2x their star level in BRICKS. BRICKS are obtained when creatures spend an action to mine, gaining BRICKS equal to their star level. 1 BRICK is awarded to each team at the end of every global turn. (See: [“FIELD CARDS”](#/rules/field-cards))
4. Points counter. Each team starts the game with 2 points. You gain points equal to your creature's star level when it dies. Points are spent on abilities, and buying items from the item shop for 4x cost their star level.
5. The Item shop. (See: [“SHOPS”](#/rules/shops))
6. The Field Card shop (See: [“SHOPS”](#/rules/shops))
7. The N.M.E. shop (See: [“SHOPS”](#/rules/shops))
:::

> [!kirby-salute] Trying to understand all of the mechanics at once can get overwhelming. Take it slow and ask your opponent for help!

1. A LANE on the PLAYING FIELD. There are three lanes. Your creatures occupy one side of the lanes, while your opponent's creatures occupy the other.
2. Your FACE-DOWN ITEMS. These items can be used at any time. (See the page on [ITEMS](#/rules/items) for more info).
3. A Slot. When occupied by a creature, this takes a turn.

6. The DISCARD PILE. Used items go here.

![The auxiliary decks and the minion card holder, numbered](table-aux.webp)

::: row 1 4
![The dice, the 0.5 chip, a counter and the stat markers, numbered](table-markers.webp)
::: panel
1. Auxillary items deck. Items summoned by specific abilities. These items are not in the main item pool, but a few items from the main pool are in this deck as they are summoned by specific creatures.
2. Auxillary Minions deck. Where all the minions summoned by specific abilities are drawn from. (See: [Minions](#/rules/minions))
3. The Minion card holder. Bring this over to the playing field and put your minion on it to keep track of summoned minion stats.
4. The Redirection cube. Use this to determine RNG.
5. The Order Determinator. Use this to determine RNG if the odds are 1 in 2.
6. 0.5 Chip incase a counter uses half integers. (See: 7.)
7. Counters, used to represent the duration of status effects, or for ability counters.

8-11:
8. Health 9. Defense 10. Attack 11. Speed markers. Use these to indicate when a creature has a stat up or down. (Flip them over for stat downs). One of these represents the actual value of a stat, not the bar. (1 DEF marker is 0.5 DEF, 1 HP marker is 1 HP)
12. Active item holder. Bring this over to the playing field and put your active item on it to keep track of its stats.

::: panel
1. This is the simple version of the game. New players will draw their creatures and items from here. More complex creatures an items are removed from these decks, so it's easier to get used to the general rules and how to play. This version of the game does not included Field Cards, or N.M.E. items. However if new players wish to play with those, they can set them up using the main deck, though it's not recommended for learning the game.
:::

## EXTRA STUFF
2. This is a copy of the deck, kept around for various reasons. In case of accidental deletion of a card, if players want to look at the full deck and discuss the game, or for certain abilities that require you to draw from a copy of the deck.
3. This bag contains specific sorted decks of items  for certain abilities that draw from specific item pools.
4. This bag contains tokens for each creature type, to signify if a creature type changes.
